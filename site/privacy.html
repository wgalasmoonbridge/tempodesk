Local-first by design

Privacy

TempoDesk keeps your time tracking data on your device. Core tracking, reports, calendar, exports, and backups work offline, without an account.

Your local data

Your entries, projects, clients, and settings are stored in a SQLite database in your operating system's application data directory. TempoDesk has no analytics, telemetry, accounts, or cloud sync.

Optional AI summaries

Local summaries stay on your device. If you choose to generate an AI summary, TempoDesk sends aggregated timesheet data for the selected range to Google Gemini using your API key. Task descriptions are included only if you enable them.

Optional live report sharing

Creating a live sharing link downloads a verified Cloudflare helper and sends your approved report through its tunnel. Sharing uses a read-only connection and only exposes the selected report data with fixed filters. Links are temporary and can be replaced. Requests stop when you stop sharing, the link expires, you quit TempoDesk, or you restore a backup.

Idle detection and machine activity

Idle detection is opt-in and local-only. It reads only the operating system's aggregate idle duration. TempoDesk does not capture screenshots, keystrokes, URLs, or window titles.

Machine activity logging is separately opt-in and local-only. It reads your OS account identity and supported session metadata, never passwords or other users' history. This ledger and its consent are excluded from portable backups, AI summaries, reports, and sharing. Restoring a backup disables logging while preserving the device-local ledger and retention preference.

Exports, backups, and deletion

CSV exports and plain-text JSON backups can contain sensitive work and client names. Store these files privately. TempoDesk does not upload backups; if you choose a folder managed by another sync service, that service controls its copies.

To remove your tracking database, close TempoDesk and delete the database file at the location shown in Settings. Save a backup first if you may need your data. Exported files and backups remain wherever you saved them.
